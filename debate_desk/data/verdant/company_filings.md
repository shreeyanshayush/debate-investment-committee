# Verdant Mobility Inc - Quarterly Filing (extract)

## Growth

Gross margin improved to 31% from 22% as hardware costs fell and software subscriptions grew to 28% of revenue.

Management raised full-year revenue guidance for the second time this year.

Cash and committed facilities of $310 million equal about 19 months of runway at the current burn rate.

## Customers

The largest customer, the European logistics group, accounted for 56% of revenue, up from 41% a year earlier.
