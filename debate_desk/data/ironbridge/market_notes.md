# Ironbridge Retail Group - Market Notes

## Valuation and income

The shares trade at 6 times forward earnings, well below the retail sector median of 12 times.

The dividend yield of 6.8% is the highest in the sector, but the payout ratio exceeds 100% of free cash flow.

A credit rating agency downgraded the company's bonds one notch to BB in October, citing weak cash generation.

## Competition and management

Two large discount competitors opened 140 new stores in Ironbridge's core regions this year, taking market share.

The new chief executive, appointed in March, has a track record of turning around two prior retailers.

Insiders purchased $4 million of shares in November following the results.
