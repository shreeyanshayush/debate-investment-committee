# Ironbridge Retail Group - FY2026 Results (extract)

## Trading

Same-store sales fell 7.4% in fiscal 2026, the third consecutive annual decline, as foot traffic continued to shrink.

The company will close 85 underperforming stores over the next 18 months, about 14% of its estate, and expects restructuring charges of $210 million.

Online sales grew 12% but represent only 9% of total revenue.

## Profitability and balance sheet

Operating margin narrowed to 3.1% from 5.6% as markdowns increased to clear excess inventory.

An inventory write-down of $85 million was recorded in the fourth quarter.

Net debt to EBITDA of 3.6x at year end, up from 2.8x a year earlier.

Free cash flow turned negative at $40 million, the second consecutive year of outflow, and the company drew $150 million on its revolving credit facility to fund the shortfall.

The cost savings programme has delivered $120 million of annualised savings, ahead of the $100 million target.
